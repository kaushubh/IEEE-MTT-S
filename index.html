1. Introduction to IEEE MTT-S

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) is a transnational society with more than 11,000 members and 190 chapters worldwide. It focuses on advancing the theory and application of RF, microwave, millimeter-wave, and terahertz technologies.

Key areas of interest include:

  • Wireless communication systems (5G/6G)
  • Radar engineering and satellite communications
  • Antenna design and wave propagation
  • Microwave integrated circuits (MMICs)

2. About MITS-DU Gwalior Chapter

The IEEE MTT-S Student Branch Chapter at MITS-DU Gwalior was established to provide students with technical exposure, hands-on workshops, and industry interaction in the fields of electronics, electromagnetics, and wireless engineering.

Our goal is to foster research, innovation, and skill development among undergraduate and postgraduate students through guest lectures, technical competitions, and project guidance.

Objectives of Our Chapter:

  1. Organize technical workshops on RF design tools (e.g., CST Studio, HFSS, MATLAB).
  2. Host industrial visits and expert guest lectures.
  3. Support student participation in national and international IEEE conferences.
  4. Encourage collaborative research and hardware project developments.

4. Domains & Activities

Members can participate and contribute across various operational and technical teams:

  • Technical & Research Team: Focuses on designing RF circuits, antenna simulations, and publishing student papers.
  • Workshop & Event Management: Coordinates logistics, venue setup, and smooth execution of chapter events.
  • Design & Media Team: Prepares promotional posters, newsletters, and social media content.
  • Public Relations & Outreach: Connects with guest speakers, industrial experts, and sister IEEE chapters.
